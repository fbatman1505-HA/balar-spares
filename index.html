What to reorder

Days of cover = stock ÷ average daily sales over the last 30 days. A line is raised when cover is shorter than the supplier lead time plus a 7-day buffer plus a 7-day warning window. Suggested order quantity covers lead time + 7 days of buffer + the next 30 days.

Purchase decisions

One line for every part the Reorder tab says is running short. The quote is compared against what this supplier has charged before, then tested against the margin that survives Amazon's fees. Buy clears the target as things stand. Reprice clears it only if the selling price goes up, and the new price is shown. Hold cannot be rescued inside your price ceiling — so the draft message negotiates instead of ordering.
