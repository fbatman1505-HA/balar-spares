What to reorder, and whether to buy it

A line is raised when days of cover — stock ÷ average daily sales over the last 30 days — falls below the supplier's lead time plus a 7-day buffer plus a 7-day warning. The suggested quantity covers lead time + 7 days + the next 30. Each line is then tested against the supplier's current quote and the margin that survives Amazon's fees: Buy clears your target as things stand, Reprice clears it only at a higher selling price, and Hold cannot be rescued inside your price ceiling — so its message negotiates instead of ordering.

Your buying policy

These three numbers decide every line below. They are yours to set, not the tool's — change one and the whole table re-decides.

This page cannot see what other sellers charge. Reading competitors' prices needs Amazon's Selling Partner API, which needs a server; scraping the site is against Amazon's terms and risks your seller account. What it does instead is work out the one number Amazon's own repricer has no way of knowing — the lowest price you can accept and still make money.

Can I match this price?

Seller Central's pricing page shows you what the Offer Display is going for. Put that figure in here and find out whether matching it leaves you anything.

The lowest you can charge

For every part: what it costs you now, what Amazon takes at your current price, and the floor — the price below which that sale stops clearing your 12% target. Set this as the minimum in Seller Central → Pricing → Automate Pricing and the free repricer can chase the Offer Display all it likes without ever taking you under water.
